# Synopsis - Momotarou Matsuri: Ishikawa Rokuemon no Maki

Built from a read of the extracted script (2026-11-01); the script decides the wording. Spellings: glossary.md.

## Setting
After Momotaro Densetsu: the oni are quiet and the villages can hold festivals again. Momotaro (silent hero) lives
with Grandpa and Grandma in Tabidachi Village. Each village festival has game stalls (minigames, quizzes, races,
fishing, Tanabata, Daimonji Bonfire...). Playing earns levels (段, up to 100) and trophies; level titles run
Master, Expert, Game Hermit. Dialect Cards and other collectibles hide around the world.

## Story (in play order, roughly by village)
1. Grandpa gets an invitation to the festival in Kachi-Kachi Village and sends Momotaro off with 100 ryo and
   millet dumplings (GSV0001D). Trophies pile up at home; Grandpa builds a shed for them.
2. A band of thieves, the **Oni Mask Gang** (鬼面党), raids villages at night. Gang members challenge Momotaro to
   games and lose (e.g. Kuritaro, the chestnut boy, at the Great Kachi-Kachi Race; Kakitaro, the persimmon boy).
   Kuritaro's big brother stole food for him after the gang robbed them; the brothers live in Brothers' Cave.
3. The gang's leader is **Ishikawa Rokuemon** (parody of the thief Ishikawa Goemon). A digger finds the
   "Revival Book of the God of Destruction": collect the four bells (Red, Blue, Yellow, Green) and go to Hell.
   Rokuemon steals it and starts taking the bells (a family heirloom, the Treasure Tower's Yellow Bell...).
4. Rokuemon kidnaps Princess Kaguya and vanishes into a hole beside the north shrine; Momotaro needs the Mark of
   Courage to reach Onigashima and Hell. Elders warn that Rokuemon "has a power no human has".
5. Gang members desert (one with the っち tic tells Momotaro the plan); the thieves reform, villages recover.
6. Climax: the God of Destruction rises as **King Bombee**, vowing to destroy everything and turn every living
   thing into a Bombee. The old Water Dragon lends Momotaro its strength (Rainbow Orb, EVT030D) to defeat him.

## Recurring people
Kintaro (Kintaro-sensei), Urashima, Yashahime, Otohime, Princess Kaguya, Netaro, Kotaro, Chuzaburo and his
sweetheart Osen, Gonbe the kimono peddler, Ginji, Enon the Poverty God, Bombee, Old Man Hanasaka, Orihime and
Hikoboshi, Karen, Yuki-onna, quiz oni (History Quiz Oni, Proverb Quiz Oni...).
